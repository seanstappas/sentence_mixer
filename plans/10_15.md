## 2. Root-Cause Summary of Remaining & Newly Introduced System Gaps

1. **Gap 1 — Unapplied Plan 3.1 Target 2 in [movie_ingest.py](file:///usr/local/google/home/seanstappas/tmp/repos/sentences/src/movie_ingest.py#L389-L407)**:
   - Plan 3.1 Target 2 proposed changing `req_margin = min(threshold, MIN_CONFIDENT_MARGIN)` to `max(...)` and requiring `and unanimous_major and beats_own`.
   - In [test_p4_adversarial.py](file:///usr/local/google/home/seanstappas/tmp/repos/sentences/tests/test_p4_adversarial.py#L255-L376) (`TestVoiceChecksBorderlineCalibration`), synthetic major speakers use orthogonal vectors with `scale=0.01` noise (`threshold ≈ 0.98`). Setting `req_margin = max(...)` raises `req_margin` to `0.98`, breaking `test_borderline_similarity_049_vs_051_single_line` (`margin = 0.51`). Requiring `and unanimous_major and beats_own` simultaneously breaks `test_minor_character_consistent_evidence_both_flagged` (where both lines of a 2-line minor speaker share the same major speaker vector, so `own_sim ≈ 1.0` and `beats_own` is `False`, while `unanimous_major` is `True`).
   - **Fix (`Patch 1`)**: Keep `req_margin = min(threshold, MIN_CONFIDENT_MARGIN) if threshold is not None else MIN_CONFIDENT_MARGIN`, define `beats_own = (best_sim - own_sim) >= MISMATCH_MARGIN and margin >= threshold`, and gate multi-line minor speaker rejection on `and (unanimous_major or beats_own)`. This catches both full-label misattribution (`unanimous_major`) and single-line major-speaker contamination inside a 2–4 line minor character (`beats_own`), while passing all 6 calibration tests in `TestVoiceChecksBorderlineCalibration`.
